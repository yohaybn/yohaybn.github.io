Privacy policy

Who we are

היום לפני.. (Today Before, com.todaybefore.yo_yo) is an Android app that shows what happened on today's date in history, published on Google Play by yo yo. Last updated: October 20, 2026. Contact: yohaybn1@gmail.com.

The app has no account and no server of its own. Your personal dates ("התאריכים שלי"), settings and reminder times are stored on your phone. The sections below list what can leave your phone, to whom, and when.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your IP address (which gives approximate location), your Android advertising ID and app set ID, ad interactions (launch, taps, views) and diagnostics (such as launch time and hangs). We do not receive this data from you directly. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). Users in the EEA and the UK are asked for consent (Google UMP) and can change it later in the app under "הגדרות פרטיות ופרסומות".

Feedback you choose to send

The feedback screen is optional. If you tap send, the app sends the text you wrote, an email address only if you type one, the app version, your device model and your Android version to a Google Form that the developer reads. We use it only to fix problems and to answer you.

Content from the web

The app requests the day's content from Hebrew Wikipedia, Wikiquote and Hebcal over HTTPS. Those requests carry only the date or page you asked for, plus the connection details (such as your IP address) that any website sees. The in-app browser is ordinary browsing of the Wikipedia pages you tap, under the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Notifications and boot

Reminders for your personal dates are local notifications set on your phone. No push service is used. The app asks to start after a restart only to set your reminders again. Permissions used: internet and network state, notifications, start on boot, plus the advertising ID and the wake lock and background work that the ads SDK adds.

Sharing

When you share a date card or add a date to your calendar, an image or an .ics file goes only to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. Android's automatic backup may save the app's settings, including personal dates, to your own Google account; that backup is handled by Android and is not sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, precise location, contacts, photos or files, calendar contents, messages, health or financial data, or browsing history. We do not sell your data. Data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Retention and deletion

Your personal dates and settings stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. Feedback is kept only as long as needed to handle it. Data held by Google for ads follows Google's own policies.

Children

היום לפני.. is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions: yohaybn1@gmail.com.
