What is RSS, in a minute

Most sites quietly publish a feed: a plain list of their latest articles. An RSS app collects those feeds from every site you add and shows them together, newest first.

So instead of opening ten tabs to see what's new, you open one app. You decide which sites are in, and that's exactly what you see.

What you get

01

One feed for all your sources

Add sites and blogs, sort them into folders, and find new sources with search.

02

A clean reader

Article text with its images, light and dark themes, LTR and RTL support.

03

Morning digest

A short summary of the latest articles from the sources you pick.

04

Read aloud

Listen to an article read aloud, with the next ones queued up. Control playback from the notification.

05

Translate articles

Translate an article into the app language and choose how it's done.

06

Same story, several sources

An optional smart scan groups similar articles into one stack. Open it and pick what to read.

07

Backup with an account

Settings and saved articles are backed up to the cloud. Or just continue as a guest.

08

Widgets

Headlines on your home screen, from all your sources or from one you pick.

Available in four languages.
